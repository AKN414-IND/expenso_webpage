Master your
money with
smart features.

Expenso combines offline privacy, intelligent AI automation, and investment tracking in a gorgeous fluid design built to run locally on your device.

  • 01

    AI Receipt Scanning

    Snap a picture of any invoice or store receipt. Expenso passes it securely to Google Gemini AI to auto-extract the merchant, date, exact total, and correct tax categories without manual entry.

    Google Gemini AI OCR Extraction Auto Categorization
  • 02

    Investments Tracker

    Monitor stocks, exchange assets, and crypto portfolios. Connects live to CoinGecko and Yahoo Finance APIs to update your net asset values, chart gains/losses, and display visual trends on modern charts.

    Yahoo Finance API CoinGecko API Live Charts
  • 03

    Interactive Heatmap

    Spot daily spending frequencies in a beautiful visual calendar grid. Plus, shake your physical phone to activate custom reanimated physics that drop virtual money coins on your screen.

    Shake Sensor Reanimated Physics Interactive Grid
  • 04

    SMS Bank Import

    Read incoming SMS notifications from banking services locally. Utilizes pattern-matching filters to read amount, vendor, and bank names, creating transactions instantly upon purchase.

    Android Exclusive Regex Parser Local Processing
  • 05

    Smart Budgeting

    Establish custom financial limits on target spending groups. Keep track of transactions in real-time, receiving prompt warnings before you overspend.

    Progress Rings Alert Limits Control
  • 06

    Subscription Detection

    Spot recurring charges automatically. Analyze payment intervals and price standard deviation patterns to find silent SaaS or app subscriptions you forgot to cancel.

    Pattern Finder Standard Deviation Algorithmic
  • 07

    Offline Sync

    Add expenses securely without network connections. Data stores locally using AsyncStorage, and automatically uploads to Supabase online tables once you're back online.

    Supabase Sync AsyncStorage NetInfo Listeners
  • 08

    Bill Alerts & Goals

    Schedule alerts for monthly rentals or utility payments, and track personal savings milestones with circular progress bars using SVG render shapes.

    Expo Notifications SVG Rings Alerts
